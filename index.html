About Elevate Coffee Academy

Elevate Coffee Academy is a professional coffee training center created to help people turn their passion for coffee into practical skills and real opportunities.

We believe that anyone can learn the art of coffee with the right training, practice, and guidance. Our training combines practical barista skills with the knowledge needed to work confidently in a professional café environment.

From espresso preparation and milk steaming to latte art, brewing methods, customer service, and café business skills, we help our students grow from beginners into confident professionals.

Our Mission

To equip aspiring baristas with practical skills, confidence, and knowledge to build a successful future in the coffee industry.
